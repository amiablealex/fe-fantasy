{{ sect(2, 'Team palette') }}

Every team in the database. The provider's hex is used for its hue only; lightness and chroma come from --team-l and --team-c, so all ten land at identical visual weight. Two stripes per team: the primary, then either a declared secondary or a lighter tint of the same hue. Edit TEAM_COLOURS in app/palette.py — a team showing neutral needs an entry. A seed may be a hex, or dark / light for a team with no hue at all, or None in the first slot to keep the provider's colour and set only a secondary. Only a hex's hue is used — the degree shown on the right is the number that matters, and the one collisions are measured in.
