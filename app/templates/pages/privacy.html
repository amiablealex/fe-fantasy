Privacy

Short version: an email address, a username and your picks. Nothing is sold, shared for marketing, or tracked.

Who is responsible

{{ config.APP_NAME }} is run by amiablealex as an individual, and is the data controller for the information described below. Contact: help@amiablealex.com.

What is held

  • Your email address. Used to sign you in and to reset your password. It is never shown to other users.
  • Your username. Visible to other people in leagues you belong to, and on the global table unless you opt out.
  • A hash of your password. The password itself is never stored and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
  • Your lineups and scores. Visible to people in a league with you, once a weekend’s deadline has passed.
  • Your IP address, in server logs and while rate-limiting sign-in and invite attempts.
  • Dates, including when you registered and when you were last active.

Why, and on what basis

Under UK GDPR every use of personal data needs a lawful basis. There are two here.

  • Performance of a contract. Your account, your lineups and your scores exist because you asked for an account and the game cannot work without them.
  • Legitimate interests. Server logs and IP-based rate limiting, to keep the service running and to stop automated sign-in and invite-code guessing. This is a limited use and you can object to it by writing to the address above.

Your data is not used for marketing or profiling, and no decisions about you are made automatically.

Who else sees it

  • Railway hosts the application and its database. The database is located in the European Union.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site and sees the IP address of every request.
  • Resend sends password reset emails, and receives your email address in order to do so. No other email is sent.

Railway, Cloudflare and Resend are companies incorporated in the United States and may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens it is covered by the standard contractual protections those providers publish. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with anyone else.

Cookies

Two, and both are strictly necessary to sign you in:

  • A session cookie, which lasts until you close your browser or sign out.
  • A “remember me” cookie, set only if you tick that box when signing in.

There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

How long it is kept

Your account and its data are kept until you delete them.

Server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept by the hosting provider for a short period — of the order of a week — and then discarded.

Database backups are taken automatically and are kept for roughly a month. Deleting your account removes it immediately from the live database; copies in existing backups disappear as those backups expire.

Your rights

You can:

  • See what is held about you, and get a copy.
  • Have anything inaccurate corrected — your username and email can be changed yourself, in account settings.
  • Delete your account, also in account settings. This removes your account, your lineups and every score computed from them.
  • Object to or restrict the limited uses listed under legitimate interests above.

Ask at help@amiablealex.com and you will get a reply within a month.

If you are not satisfied with the answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK regulator.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 16 or over. See the terms.

Changes
