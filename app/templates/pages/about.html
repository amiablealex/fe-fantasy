About

A fantasy game for the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship. Free to play, non-commercial, run by one person.

What it is

Pick four drivers and a team. They score points from real race weekends across the season. One transfer per weekend, banked up to two.

Leagues are private and joined by invite. Everyone also appears in one global table, which you can opt out of.

How to play covers the rules and the scoring.

Who made it

Built by amiablealex as a personal project.

There is a companion app, F1 Predictions. The two share an author and nothing else.

Where the results come from

Formula E timing and results come from the Orange Cat Blacktop API. Sessions are fetched shortly after they finish and scored automatically, so qualifying scores usually appear on the day and the rest arrives with the race.

A weekend that has not finished scoring says so on the page rather than showing a total as final.

What it does not do

No email except a password reset. No deadline reminders, no digests, no notifications.

No adverts, no tracking, no analytics.

No entry fee, no prizes, no money of any kind.

Not official

This is an unofficial fan project with no connection to Formula E Operations, the FIA, ABB, or any team, driver or sponsor. None of them have endorsed or approved it.

Names including “Formula E”, “E-Prix” and the names of teams and drivers are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used here only where necessary to describe the championship this game is played on.
