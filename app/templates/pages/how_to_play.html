How to play

Pick four drivers and a team. They score from real Formula E weekends. One transfer per weekend, banked up to two.

Your lineup

Four drivers and one team, subject to two rules:

  • No two of your drivers from the same team.
  • Your team pick must not be a team you already have a driver from.

Change it as often as you like until the deadline. After that it is locked.

The deadline

One per weekend, at the first qualifying session. A double-header has one deadline covering both races.

Transfers

One per weekend, banked up to a maximum of two.

A transfer is one changed slot: a driver for a driver, or your team for another team.

Bringing in a driver from the team you hold forces your team pick to move as well. Two slots change, so it costs two transfers, and you need both banked before it is available.

Everything is free until the first deadline of the season. Transfers start counting from the second weekend.

Scoring

Scored per round. A double-header scores your lineup twice.

Qualifying

Reaching the Duels — top four of your group {{ q.group_progress }}
Winning a duel — quarter-final, semi-final, final {{ q.duel_win }} each
Pole{{ q.pole }}

Which gives {{ pole_total }} for pole, {{ q.group_progress + 2 * q.duel_win }} for losing the final, {{ q.group_progress + q.duel_win }} for losing a semi-final, {{ q.group_progress }} for losing a quarter-final, and nothing for going out in the groups.

Pole is the driver who wins the Qual Final. A grid penalty moves a driver back but does not take the pole away.

Race

Win{{ r.win }}
Podium{{ r.podium }}
Points finish — top ten{{ r.points_finish }}
Fastest lap of the race{{ r.fastest_lap }}
Every {{ r.places_step }} places gained +{{ r.places_gained_per_step }}, up to +{{ r.places_gained_cap }}
Every {{ r.places_step }} places lost −{{ r.places_lost_per_step }}, down to −{{ r.places_lost_cap }}

These stack. A win scores {{ r.win }} + {{ r.podium }} + {{ r.points_finish }} before places gained and fastest lap.

The fastest lap point goes to whoever set the quickest lap, wherever they finished.

Places gained and lost are measured from where a driver started, after any penalty. Retirements are classified at the back of the field.

Your team pick

Half of what its two drivers score that round, including anything negative. Halves are kept rather than rounded: drivers on 8 and 3 give the team 5.5.

Twentieth in qualifying, finishes P11 {{ r.places_gained_per_step }}

Drivers who do not appear

A driver who does not take part in a session scores nothing for it. There is no substitute, and no free transfer if a driver leaves the grid.

Who can see your lineup

Nobody, until the deadline passes. After that it is visible to people in a league with you.

The Perfect Five

After a weekend has been scored you can see what it was worth: the four best-scoring drivers and the best-scoring team. The lineup rules do not apply to it, so it is not necessarily a lineup that could have been picked.
